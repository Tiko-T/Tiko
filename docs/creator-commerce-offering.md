# Tiko Creator Commerce Offering

## 1. What Tiko Is

Tiko is a commerce platform for creators, event organizers, brands, and communities that need to sell access, experiences, and digital offerings through one product.

At its core, Tiko combines two things:

- **ticketing**, for selling and managing event access
- **creator commerce**, for selling the broader products and perks that surround a creator, audience, or campaign

The product is designed to feel like a polished Web2 storefront and operations platform. Buyers should be able to discover, purchase, and use what they bought through a familiar web experience. Behind the scenes, blockchain is used only where it improves ownership, provenance, authenticity, and fan relationships.

Tiko is not just a ticket scanner or a crypto marketplace. It is a system for selling access, building audience relationships, and extending a creator's commercial offering beyond the event itself.

## 2. What Exists Today

Today, Tiko already supports the ticketing foundation of the product:

- event listing and publishing
- ticket purchase flow
- wallet-based payment confirmation on CKB testnet
- order tracking and payment status
- ticket issuance after confirmed payment
- QR-based ticket access
- operator check-in
- buyer wallet linking
- Spore-backed digital ownership issued after successful purchase

This means the platform already handles the most operationally sensitive part of the experience: taking payment, issuing access, and validating attendance.

In practical terms, Tiko already works as a modern event ticketing system with blockchain-backed proof of ownership in the background.

## 3. What We Are About to Build

The next stage is to turn Tiko from a ticketing-first product into a fuller creator commerce platform. The ticketing system remains the base, but we expand the product offering around it so creators can sell more than entry.

The seven best-fit creator-commerce components for Tiko are:

1. digital drops
2. memberships and passes
3. limited editions and collectibles
4. post-purchase fan rewards
5. gated access products
6. physical merchandise with authenticity
7. bundles

Together, these give creators and event operators a broader revenue model, a stronger fan relationship model, and a more distinctive offering than a standard ticketing product alone.

## 4. The Expanded Product Offering

### 4.1 Ticketing

Ticketing remains the core operational layer of the platform.

Tiko helps organizers:

- publish live events
- sell paid or free tickets
- issue access credentials
- manage check-in on event day
- keep operational control over validity, support, and entry

For buyers, this means:

- browsing events normally
- purchasing through a standard web flow
- paying at the final step with the supported wallet
- receiving a usable mobile ticket quickly
- keeping proof of purchase and ownership after the event

This is the part of Tiko that already works.

### 4.2 Digital Drops

Digital drops extend the platform from event access into creator-led digital merchandise.

These can include:

- posters and artwork
- behind-the-scenes content
- digital editions
- premium media
- templates, downloads, and exclusive assets

This gives creators a way to sell products that do not depend on physical attendance while still benefiting from the same checkout, fulfillment, and ownership model that ticketing already uses.

### 4.3 Memberships and Passes

Memberships turn Tiko into an ongoing access platform, not just a one-time purchase platform.

Examples include:

- fan club access
- VIP passes
- season access
- community memberships
- backstage or partner badges

This allows creators and communities to build recurring or longer-term relationships with their audience instead of relying only on individual ticket sales or isolated campaigns.

### 4.4 Limited Editions and Collectibles

Limited editions give creators a high-value product category that benefits directly from blockchain-backed provenance.

These can include:

- collectible event editions
- numbered campaign items
- limited creator drops
- premium memorabilia
- proof-of-attendance objects with collectible value

For creators, this means more scarcity-led revenue opportunities. For buyers, it means portable proof of ownership that feels special beyond the immediate transaction.

### 4.5 Post-Purchase Fan Rewards

Post-purchase fan rewards help Tiko become a retention product instead of only a transaction product.

These rewards can include:

- proof-of-attendance collectibles
- follow-up drops for event buyers
- bonus content for members
- exclusive perks for repeat supporters
- targeted reward campaigns tied to prior purchases

This makes the platform valuable after the sale, which is especially important for creators who want to grow long-term fan value rather than simply sell entry once.

### 4.6 Gated Access Products

Gated access products expand the meaning of ownership and access beyond event admission.

Examples include:

- members-only content
- event replay access
- holder-only pages or products
- early-access product unlocks
- premium community experiences

This gives creators a flexible way to package value around access, status, and belonging while keeping the actual access rules controlled by the platform.

### 4.7 Physical Merchandise with Authenticity

Physical merchandise with authenticity gives Tiko a path into premium creator and brand commerce.

Examples include:

- signed items
- limited apparel
- collectible goods
- premium merch drops
- event merchandise tied to digital proof

The commercial value here is not just selling a physical item. It is selling a physical item with trusted provenance and a stronger ownership story.

### 4.8 Bundles

Bundles connect all of the above into higher-value offers.

Examples include:

- ticket + digital drop
- ticket + collectible
- membership + exclusive content
- event access + replay + premium merchandise

Bundles are especially important because they turn ticketing into a broader commerce engine. Instead of selling one item, creators can sell a fuller package around the event, the campaign, or the fan relationship.

## 5. Why This Offering Matters

The product opportunity for Tiko is not only that it can sell tickets.

The stronger opportunity is that it can help creators and operators:

- monetize before the event
- monetize at the event
- monetize after the event
- reward attendance
- build longer-lived audience relationships
- create more premium products with stronger trust signals

This is what makes creator commerce a meaningful extension of the product instead of a side feature.

Ticketing brings urgency, operational utility, and real transaction volume. Creator commerce brings repeatability, audience retention, and higher-value product categories. Together, they create a more complete platform offering.

## 6. What Makes Tiko Different

Tiko should not present itself as a crypto product first.

Its differentiation should be:

- a familiar buying experience
- a strong event operations layer
- creator-ready commerce beyond tickets
- digital ownership where it adds real value
- better trust, portability, and post-purchase engagement than traditional platforms

In simple terms:

**Tiko helps creators and event operators sell access, merchandise, memberships, and fan experiences through one platform, while using blockchain only where it meaningfully improves ownership and authenticity.**
